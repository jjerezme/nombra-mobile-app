Play limits: title 30, short description 80, full description 4000, release notes 500 characters. Draft: review before publishing.

## Title

Nombra: download and rename

## Short description

Download files and save them with the name your rules define.

## Full description

Nombra downloads a file from a link, or takes one you share from another app, and saves it with the name your rules produce.

RENAME RULES
• A pattern with the original name, date, time, running number, domain and type.
• Letter case, spaces and name cleanup.
• Destination folder and what to do if the file already exists.
• Quick mode: just a prefix and a suffix.

DOWNLOADS
• In the background, with progress and retries.
• Only https links.
• Saved to Download, Documents, Pictures, Movies or Music.

SHARING
• "Save with Nombra" appears when you share a file or a link from another app.

YOUR DATA
• Works without an account. If you want, sign in with Google to back up your rules.
• No ads and no usage analytics.
• You can delete your account from the app.
